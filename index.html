Jeongyeon Choi

Master's Candidate, Library and Information Science

I am a master’s student in the Department of Library and Information Science at Yonsei University, studying under the supervision of Professor Yongjun Zhu at DataLab. I recently completed a dual master’s degree program in Library and Information Science at Simmons University with a concentration in Information Science and Technology.

I primarily work in the areas of science of science, mental health informatics, and computational social science. Using methods from data science and computational analysis, I study large-scale datasets such as academic publications, bibliometric records, and social media content to explore how knowledge is shared and used in digital environments.
